5/ Funding Rates Re-engineered:
Unlike conventional 8-hour funding models, Aeonian updates funding per-second dynamically:

$F = \text{clamp}\left(\frac{P_{\text{mark}} - P_{\text{index}}}{P_{\text{index}}}, -0.05\%, +0.05\%\right) \times \frac{\Delta t}{3600}$

Arbitrageurs are incentivized to maintain parity instantly, keeping delta risk minimal. [6/14]
